Between-session coaching support

Your coaching shouldn't pause when the session ends.

The most important shifts often happen after the call, when clients return to real life, familiar patterns, and competing demands. IBE keeps your methodology present between sessions, helping clients stay connected to the work while giving you a clear view of what needs attention next.

How IBE works

Four agents work the space between sessions.

The Mirror Agent talks with your client in the moment. The other three work in the background between sessions, so the live conversation stays focused.

The Mirror Agent

Listens without directing. Asks one clear question at a time and never offers advice, so what comes up is the client's own thinking.

The Pattern Agent

Reviews the conversation and notices language patterns that can signal avoidance, such as “should” or “have to.”

The Challenge Agent

Turns what it found into a touchpoint in your method, such as a 48-hour field experiment or a daily nudge.

The Integration Agent

Hands you a brief, not a transcript: a 60-second read of the session, language patterns, and touchpoint before your next call.

Your methodology, not a generic model

The coach's IP drives everything.

Your framework, questions, and language load into the system as configuration. The agents work from what you have already built, so support is shaped by your method rather than a generic chatbot script.
